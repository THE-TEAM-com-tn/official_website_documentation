playground: "simple"
---

The article counterpart of the [blog share page](/api-reference/web/share-blog). Crawlers read real Open
Graph, Twitter and `article:*` tags (published and modified times, section, tags); people are redirected at
once to `https://theteam.com.tn/{slug}`, or `https://theteam.com.tn/en/{slug}` with `?lang=en`.

- Title and description are the article's resolved SEO fields.
- `og:locale` is `fr_FR` or `en_GB`.
- Unknown, scheduled or unpublished slugs redirect (`302`) to the Knowledge Corner listing (`/articles` or
  `/en/articles`).

<ParamField path="slug" type="string" required>
  Article slug.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="lang" type="string" default="fr">
  `fr` or `en`.
</ParamField>

<RequestExample>

```bash cURL
curl "https://admin.theteam.com.tn/share/article/certification-pmp-version-8?lang=en"
```

</RequestExample>
