Knowledge Corner articles are long-form SEO pages. The site serves each one **at its root**
(`https://theteam.com.tn/{slug}`, English `/en/{slug}`) and lists them on the `/articles` tab of the
resource hub. Article slugs can never equal a top-level route of the site: the backend refuses reserved
slugs.

| Method | Path | Description |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| `GET` | [`/articles`](/api-reference/articles/list) | Published articles with search, category and type filters |
| `GET` | [`/articles/filters`](/api-reference/articles/filters) | Categories and resource types with their counts |
| `GET` | [`/articles/pinned`](/api-reference/articles/pinned) | Up to 10 pinned articles |
| `GET` | [`/articles/{slug}`](/api-reference/articles/get) | One article with its HTML content and up to 3 related articles |

- An article is visible once `status` is on and its `published_at` date is **reached**: a future date
  schedules it.
- Resource types: `article`, `guide`, `tutorial`, `case_study`, `glossary`, with labels in the request language.
- Articles share their categories and tags with blog posts.
- Crawlers use [`/share/article/{slug}`](/api-reference/web/share-article) and
  [`/sitemap-articles.xml`](/api-reference/web/sitemap-articles).
