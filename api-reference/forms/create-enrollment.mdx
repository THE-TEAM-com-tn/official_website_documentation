---
title: "Enroll in a training"
sidebarTitle: "Enroll in a training"
description: "Register one or more seats on the next session of a published training."
api: "POST /enrollments"
playground: "simple"
---

Backs the enrollment form of the course page. The enrollment attaches to the training itself (there is
no session table) and staff confirm it in the admin panel. The training title is copied into
`training_title`, so the record stays readable if the training is renamed. Every staff account gets a
notification. No email is sent.

Only **published** trainings accept enrollments. This endpoint is not rate-limited.

## Body

<ParamField body="training_id" type="integer" required>
  Id of a published training (from [List trainings](/api-reference/trainings/list)).
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="contact_name" type="string" required>
  At most 191 characters.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="contact_email" type="string" required>
  Valid email, at most 191 characters.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="contact_phone" type="string" required>
  At most 191 characters.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="customer_type" type="string" default="private">
  `company` or `private`. `null` counts as missing.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="seats" type="integer" default="1">
  Between 1 and 500. `null` counts as missing.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="internal_notes" type="string">
  Free text from the visitor (preferred dates, constraints…).
</ParamField>

## Response

<ResponseField name="message" type="string" required>`Votre inscription a bien été enregistrée.`</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="data" type="object" required>
  The stored enrollment: the fields above plus `id`, `training_title`, `is_confirmed` (`false`),
  `is_archived` (`false`), `created_at`, `updated_at`, and `training`, the **full training record** as
  stored, internal columns included (`featured_image_path`, `status`, `elearning_unlock_rule`…).
</ResponseField>

## Errors

- `422` with Laravel's default shape when a field is invalid or the id does not exist.
- `422` with `{"message": "Certains champs sont invalides.", "errors": {"training_id": ["La formation sélectionnée n'est pas disponible."]}}`
  when the training exists but is not published.

```bash cURL
    "contact_name": "Sami Ben Ali",
    "contact_email": "sami.benali@example.com",

    "customer_type": "company",
    "seats": 3,
    "internal_notes": "Sessions le week-end de préférence."
  }'
```
