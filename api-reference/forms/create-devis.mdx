title: "Request a quote (devis)"
sidebarTitle: "Request a quote"
description: "Send a quote request for one or more trainings, as a company or an individual."
api: "POST /devis"
playground: "simple"
---

Backs the site's `/devis` page. The request is stored for the staff (**Inbox → Devis**), the address joins
the newsletter list with source `devis` (unless already there), every staff account gets a notification,
and the team inbox receives an email whose `Reply-To` is the requester.

Server-side rules on top of validation:

- An `individual` request has `company_name` and `participants_count` **forced to `null`**.
- Training titles are **resolved on the server** from published trainings. Ids that are not published are
  dropped silently, and titles are never taken from the client.
- A failed email does not fail the request: the response says `"email_sent": false`.

Rate limit: **6 requests per minute**.

## Body

<ParamField body="client_type" type="string" required>
  `company` or `individual`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="company_name" type="string">
  Required when `client_type` is `company`. At most 191 characters.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="participants_count" type="integer">
  Required when `client_type` is `company`. Between 1 and 10,000.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="full_name" type="string" required>
  At most 191 characters.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="email" type="string" required>
  Valid email (RFC), at most 191 characters.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="phone" type="string">
  At most 50 characters.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="training_ids" type="integer[]">
  Up to 20 training ids, from [List training options](/api-reference/trainings/options).
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="message" type="string">
  At most 5,000 characters.
</ParamField>

## Response

<ResponseField name="message" type="string" required>`Votre demande de devis a bien été envoyée.`</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="email_sent" type="boolean" required>Whether the team email went out.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="reference" type="integer" required>Id of the stored request, to quote in follow-ups.</ResponseField>

<RequestExample>

```bash cURL
curl -X POST "https://admin.theteam.com.tn/api/devis" \
  -H "Accept: application/json" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "client_type": "company",
    "company_name": "ACME SARL",
    "participants_count": 12,
    "full_name": "Sami Ben Ali",
    "email": "sami.benali@example.com",
    "phone": "+216 20 000 000",
    "training_ids": [5, 6],
    "message": "Formation souhaitée en présentiel à Tunis."
  }'
```

```javascript JavaScript
const res = await fetch("https://admin.theteam.com.tn/api/devis", {
  method: "POST",
  headers: { Accept: "application/json", "Content-Type": "application/json" },
  body: JSON.stringify({
    client_type: "individual",
    full_name: "Sami Ben Ali",
    email: "sami.benali@example.com",
    training_ids: [5],
  }),
});
if (res.status === 429) showRetryLater();
```

</RequestExample>
