<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I edited a training but the website still shows the old version.">
    That is normal: the website keeps the training catalogue in memory for **up to 5 minutes** so it loads faster. Wait a few minutes, then reload the website page.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I published a training: when will it show up on Google?">
    Visitors see it right away. Google, however, reads a version of the page prepared each time the **website is put online**: after publishing a training, changing its title or slug, or unpublishing it, tell the person who puts the website online so they publish it again. Google then usually takes a few days to update its results. See [Trainings](/en/guide-utilisateur/formations).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My article is saved but does not appear on the website.">
    Check in the record that **Published** is on and that the **Published at** date is not in the future (otherwise the article is « Scheduled » and will go out on that date). In the list of Knowledge Corner articles, the **Status** column shows Live, Scheduled or Draft.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I cannot see a « Delete » button.">
    Only **administrators** can delete. Content managers can **archive**, which puts the item away without losing it. For devis, messages and reclamations, even an administrator must archive first before deleting.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I cannot edit some fields of the landing page.">
    Fields marked **ADMINS ONLY** can only be edited by an administrator. The content manager can edit the **promo banner (top strip)** and the **banner**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The English (EN) field is empty: is that a problem?">
    No. English is optional: the English version of the website then shows the French text. Fill in the **EN** tab when a translation is available.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I cannot hear the sound for new requests.">
    The browser only allows sound after a first click on the page. Open the bell and click **Test**. Then leave a tab of the panel open, even in the background. See [Notifications](/en/guide-utilisateur/notifications).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I do not get notification pop-ups on my computer.">
    Open the bell: if « Browser notifications » shows **Enable**, click it then **Allow**; if it shows **Blocked**, allow the site again in the browser settings. Also check that your computer's « Do not disturb » mode is off. Step by step: [Notifications](/en/guide-utilisateur/notifications).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I forgot my password.">
    On the sign-in page, click **Forgot your password?** and follow the link you receive by email. See [Signing in](/en/guide-utilisateur/connexion).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I lost my phone (two-factor authentication).">
    When signing in, use one of your **recovery codes** instead of the 6-digit code. If you no longer have them, ask an administrator for help.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The invitation link no longer works.">
    It is only valid for **24 hours** and works only once. Ask an administrator to **resend** the invitation.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Signing in is blocked after several attempts.">
    After 5 failed attempts within one minute, signing in is blocked for a short while. Wait a minute, then try again or reset your password.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A client did not receive my reply to their reclamation.">
    Check that the reply was actually **saved**, then click **Notify client by email**. Ask the client to look in their junk mail. The client can also read your reply by tracking their complaint on the website with their tracking number.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The website's forms no longer arrive by email.">
    An administrator can check the address in **Website Settings → Notifications** and send a test email. Requests remain visible in the panel's **Inbox** in any case. See [Log and settings](/en/guide-utilisateur/journal-et-parametres).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
