<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I can't find the “Delete” button.">
    That is normal: only administrators can delete. You can **archive** a request (it stays visible with the “Archived” filter) or **unpublish** content to take it off the website. For a permanent deletion, ask an administrator.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Some fields on the landing page are locked.">
    Fields marked **ADMINS ONLY** can only be edited by an administrator. You can edit the **promo banner** and the **banner strip**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I don't see “Users” or “Activity Log” in the menu.">
    These parts are reserved for administrators. To create an account for a colleague, change a role or view the history of changes, contact an administrator.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I edited a training but the website hasn't changed.">
    The website keeps the training catalog in memory for **up to 5 minutes**. Wait a little, then reload the website page.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My article doesn't appear on the website.">
    Check that **Published** is turned on and that the **Published at** date is not in the future (otherwise the article is “Scheduled”).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I can't hear the sound for new requests.">
    Open the bell and click **Test**, then keep a panel tab open. If “Browser notifications” shows **Blocked**, follow the [Notifications](/en/guide-utilisateur/gestionnaire/notifications) page.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I want to change the email address that receives the forms.">
    It is reserved for administrators (Website Settings → Notifications). Ask an administrator.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I forgot my password.">
    On the sign-in page, click **Forgot your password?** and follow the link you receive by email.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My invitation link no longer works.">
    It is only valid for **24 hours**. Ask an administrator to send it again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I don't know what the visitor saw when filling in the form.">
    The **Visitor side** tab of this guide shows each form on the website, as it appears to visitors.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
